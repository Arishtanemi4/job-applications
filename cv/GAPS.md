## Clearance and eligibility (cannot be closed by a project)

- **SC (Security Check) clearance - Rowden, AI Solutions Architect.** The JD lists "Candidates must be eligible for SC clearance" as an essential requirement. SC normally needs **5 years of continuous UK residency** immediately before applying (3 years for CTC/Level 1B, 10 for DV), and BPSS, which gates all of them, needs roughly 3 years of UK history as well. UK residency currently runs from Sep 2025 (MSc at Bristol; HDFC was in India until Jul 2025), so about 1 year. Nationality is not the bar in itself - Commonwealth nationals are routinely cleared - and there is no project to build here: the gap closes with time plus a sponsor and a long-term visa route, and the Graduate Visa (2 years) does not outlast a five-year residency clock. Earliest realistic point is roughly Sep 2030. **Rowden was not applied for.** Per the rule in `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md`, clearance roles get no entry in `ApplicationTracker.xlsx` at all, so Rowden and CGI have both been dropped from the sheet.
- Sources: Home Office careers (states 5 years for SC), UKSV "Vetting explained" (who may apply; notes that relatively few posts are reserved to UK nationals), UKSV "clearance levels" (what each check involves). The SC guidance pack for applicants describes the process but does not state the residency figure, which is why it is not the useful source here. Per the Security Clearance rule in `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md`, any future JD that requires clearance is a hard stop: no CV, no cover letter and no tracker entry - only the gap gets recorded here.
