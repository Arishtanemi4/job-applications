We trade online, advise businesses on digital transformation and data-driven marketing, and are developing an AI-powered automation platform that turns repetitive processes into scalable systems.We're small and early-stage, which means the person in this role will see the whole business rather than one corner of it.The roleWe're building an agentic automation platform that replaces repetitive operational work with systems. This role is hands-on building and testing those workflows — not research, not prompt tinkering. You'll ship things other people depend on.You'll work directly with the leadership team on what gets built and why.What you'll do
Build and test automation workflows using LLM APIs

Integrate with third-party tools and APIs across our ventures
Write evaluations — decide how we know a workflow is working and catch it when it isn't
Take workflows from prototype to reliable enough to run unattended
Document what you build so someone else can pick it up
