The opportunity 

The Efficiency and Automation team is a multi-disciplinary team responsible for streamlining business processes, reducing administrative burden and enhancing operational efficiency across the Bank. Through a combination of process automation, intelligent automation, agentic workflows and Generative AI, the team delivers frequent, high-impact improvements that enable colleagues to focus more time on value-adding activities and customer service.

As automation technologies continue to evolve, the team is expanding its use of Agentic Automation, Predictive and Generative AI capabilities to automate increasingly complex processes, enhance decision-making and improve employee and customer experiences.

The Bank's strategy is centred around delivering operational efficiencies that create capacity to support increased business volumes and profitability, while improving customer satisfaction and outcomes. The Efficiency and Automation team supports this strategy by identifying and delivering small, frequent improvements that drive measurable business value and accelerate digital transformation.

Key responsibilities 

As an AI Engineer you will design build and maintain the software foundations that enable AI use cases to move from idea to production. You will work across backend engineering, data integration, model integration, APIs, orchestration, monitoring and deployment. The role requires strong software engineering discipline and practical experience building and productionise AI use cases from machine learning models to GenAI systems whilst ensuring they are safely deployed.

You will work closely with Product Managers and Data Scientists and should be comfortable working through the phases of the product development cycle end-to-end: understanding the business problem, shaping the technical approach, integrating data sources, building services, connecting models or LLMs, implementing controls and supporting solutions after release.

Design, build and maintain backend services, APIs and orchestration layers that support AI and data-driven use cases across the bank.
Develop robust, production-grade software using modern engineering practices, including clean code, automated testing, version control and secure development standards.
Integrate AI models, machine learning services and GenAI capabilities into banking workflows, CRM systems and Bank Manager facing applications.
Build and maintain data pipelines, feature pipelines and service integrations required to support AI products.
Support the deployment and operation of models and AI services, including monitoring, logging, alerting, performance management and incident response.
Work with Data Scientists to productionise models, expose model outputs through reliable services and ensure outputs are usable by business applications.
Implement safeguards for AI use cases, audit trails and access controls.
Collaborate with Risk, Compliance, Security and Architecture teams to ensure AI solutions meet internal governance, regulatory and operational resilience expectations.
Contribute to reusable engineering patterns, shared components, reference architectures and deployment standards for AI-enabled solutions.
Support experimentation and prototyping while ensuring successful use cases can scale into maintainable, production-ready solutions.
Contribute to a high-quality engineering culture focused on pragmatism, collaboration, learning and delivery.
