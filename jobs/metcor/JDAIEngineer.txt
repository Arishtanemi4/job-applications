How we work We define the KPIs before we build, not after. Every project states what it is supposed to move and by how much, we track it during development, and we hold ourselves accountable afterwards whether or not it flattered us. If a feature did not do what we said it would, we iterate. This is the part of our culture we are most deliberate about, and the part you will have the most influence over.
