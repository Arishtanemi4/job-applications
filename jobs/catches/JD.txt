About the job

One of 30 new companies, from more than 25,000, selected by NVIDIA for its GTC 2026 event in San Jose. Investors include Antoine Arnault, Natalia Vodianova, Roy Chung (Apollo.io), Dillon Erb (Paperspace) and Sarah Willersdorf (former Head of Luxury, BCG).
Location: Remote
Job Summary:
AI Engineer role focussed on building a new consumer-facing agentic experience at CATCHES.This is an opportunity to combine modern agentic systems with proprietary data and foundational technology developed in-house to create a unique product experience.You’ll work directly with the founders and engineering team to design, build and ship the systems behind the product, taking new capabilities from prototype through to production.
Responsibilities:
Agentic workflows: tool use, planning, routing, multi-step execution and proactive behaviour
Agent harness: build and evolve context management, structured outputs, retries, fallbacks, compaction and long-running sessions
State, memory and retrieval: durable state, memory schemas, reconciliation, forgetting, keyword/vector/hybrid retrieval
Orchestration: subagents, multi-agent systems, managed runtimes vs custom harnesses, durable workflows
Tools and integrations: MCP, tool gateways, third-party services, browser/computer use, messaging and multimodal inputs
Decisioning and control: model/tool routing, guardrails and calibrated decision models
Evals and observability: task success, regressions, failure modes, tracing and production monitoring
Production: reliability, security, multi-user isolation, latency, cost and model serving

Requirements:
Strong software engineering foundations ideally across backend or full-stack engineering
Experience shipping agentic or LLM-powered product experiences to real users
Hands-on experience building agent orchestration, tool use and multi-step workflows
Strong understanding of context engineering, state, memory and long-running agent systems
Experience designing evals and diagnosing agent failure modes
Experience with tracing, observability and debugging non-deterministic systems
Comfortable integrating models, APIs, third-party services and product infrastructure
Strong product judgement and the ability to move quickly from experimentation into production
Comfortable evaluating rapidly changing models, frameworks and approaches

Working at CATCHES:
Remote with optional co-working access.
Be part of an experienced team that values shipping, experimentation, and autonomy.
