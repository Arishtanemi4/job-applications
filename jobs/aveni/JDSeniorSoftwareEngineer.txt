Overview
Application
Aveni is redefining how financial institutions use AI. We build real, production-ready agentic systems that reshape how banks, advisers, and financial services firms operate, replacing fragmented processes with intelligent, end-to-end workflows. Our achievements include being recognised as Fintech of the Year for two consecutive years and winning Best Specialist Technology Service at the Professional Adviser Awards 2025.

As the adoption of AI accelerates in financial services, we’re scaling our team to meet growing demand.

 
The Role
We're seeking a hands-on Senior AI Software Engineer with strong full-stack experience to build and ship AI-powered products into production, leveraging FinLLM (the UK's first LLM for Financial Services) and other LLMs. You won’t build or train models—instead, you'll engineer the systems, services, interfaces, and integrations that bring those models to life for advisers, analysts, and banking teams. You'll own features end-to-end, covering selection, implementation, deployment, observability, and iteration across backend, frontend, and cloud infrastructure.

 
Key Responsibilities
Build production-ready, AI-powered products integrating LLM capabilities, RAG, and agentic workflows into real-world processes.

Select models, orchestrate prompts, design systems, and implement AI tooling (e.g., LangGraph, Langfuse).

Design evaluation, monitoring, and observability to ensure reliability and readiness for production.

Develop scalable, event-driven microservices and APIs using Node.js and TypeScript.

Build modern, responsive React frontends to make AI useful in customer workflows.

Architect cloud-native systems on AWS (Lambda, ECS/Fargate, API Gateway, SQS/SNS, DynamoDB, RDS).

Implement microservices, distributed-system principles, and strong operational controls for integration into financial products.

Uphold engineering best practices: clean code, automated testing, CI/CD, and operational excellence.

Diagnose and improve production behaviours relating to reliability, latency, cost, and AI quality.

Mentor engineers, contribute to team growth, and collaborate closely with product and engineering to deliver customer-ready features.
