The role

Build the AI systems at the heart of a platform that the world's leading PE firms run their investment processes on. Ownership: You'll own agent capabilities end to end: from the harness internals to the evals that prove they work. You'll make the design calls, ship them, and operate them.

Growth: You'll work across the entire stack, alongside a team of startup veterans, and take on real responsibility.

Upside: You'll receive meaningful equity in a company that grew 15x last year.

Recent projects include

Agent Harness: Our own agent harness built on a custom, streaming-first graph execution framework.

Code Execution Framework: Our own code execution framework that can be interrupted and resumed mid-execution for programmatic tool calling.

Document Ingestion, Indexing, and Search: A VLM-based OCR pipeline, a self-hosted search cluster, and domain-aware search strategies.
