Overview
We are looking for an AI Engineer to design, develop and deploy AI-powered solutions that drive business value and improve operational efficiency. This role will focus on building and optimising machine learning models, generative AI applications, and intelligent automation solutions while working closely with technical and business stakeholders.

The successful candidate will combine strong software engineering skills with practical experience in AI technologies, helping to shape and deliver the organisation's AI strategy.

Design, build, and deploy AI and machine learning solutions in production environments.
Develop and optimise Large Language Model (LLM) applications.
Create AI-powered copilots, chatbots, knowledge management solutions, and workflow automation tools.
Build and maintain scalable data pipelines to support AI initiatives.
Implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) frameworks and vector databases.
Integrate AI technologies into existing business systems, applications, and processes.
Evaluate emerging AI technologies and recommend solutions that deliver measurable business benefits.
Monitor and improve model performance, accuracy, reliability, and security.
Collaborate with data engineers, software developers, and business stakeholders to gather requirements and deliver solutions.
Establish AI governance, best practices, and responsible AI standards.
Provide technical guidance and support to internal teams on AI adoption and implementation.
