Job Description

In AECOM’s AI Engineering team your code will directly shape the physical world around us. We build AI-driven technology that revolutionises how infrastructure and buildings are designed and engineered; reducing waste, cutting CO₂, and making the built environment more efficient and sustainable. This is where software has measurable, real-world impact.

With our AI Engineering team we’ve created a unique setup: a lean, highly technical team with the speed and ownership of a start up, backed by the scale, resources, and domain expertise of one of the world’s leading engineering firms. 

What You’ll Do

As part of our AI Engineering team, you will operate at the intersection of technology, product, and business - shaping how AI is applied across all of AECOM. You will turn ideas into validated solution concepts, helping the organization understand what is possible, valuable, and feasible.

Identify and shape AI opportunities across the business

Assess feasibility by balancing technical capability, business value, and complexity

Design and build prototypes, demos, and MVPs

Translate problems into structured approaches and PRDs

Collaborate with engineers, product managers, and domain experts

Explore emerging AI models and tools

Support the lifecycle from concept to production handover

Operate flexibly across demos, prototyping, and exploration

As a AI Solution Engineer you might run a demo, support operational challenges, explore the latest LLM capabilities, join workshops, make discovery calls, and sometimes just spend a full day building PoCs and MVPs. No two days are the same

Qualifications

Must-Have Qualifications

Degree in computer science, software engineering, innovation/entrepreneurship/technology or a related technical field or equivalent demonstrable experience

Background in AI, engineering, data, product or consulting (mindset matters more than title)

Understanding of AI/LLM capabilities and limitations, and familiarity with AI tooling (LLMs, APIs, MCPs, RAG systems)

Ability to build prototypes using Python, web frameworks and similar tools, with good understanding of the idea-prototype-production lifecycle

Experience translating ideas into structured approaches or prototypes, assess feasibility and communicate trade-offs

Preferred Skills

Experience working with senior stakeholders to shape solutions

Experience translating ideas into structured approaches or prototypes

High-level understanding of cloud platforms, containers, and backend system

Exposure to finance, operations, or commercial work
