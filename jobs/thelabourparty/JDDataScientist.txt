The Labour Party is looking to recruit a Data Scientist. The post-holder will be responsible for conducting polling, advanced modelling, and voter targeting and analysing public opinion, voting behaviour, and campaign performance to contribute to Labour’s political and organisational strategies.

 

The successful candidate will have:

 

Strong understanding of statistical modelling, with a minimum of degree-level statistical understanding, or equivalent professional experience
Strong understanding of polling methodology and how to use to polling to answer political questions.
Experience of conducting analysis and reporting on political data and polling and producing outputs like reports, visualisations and dashboards for stakeholders.
Ability to understand how data on voters, campaigns and elections can be used to drive strategic decision making
