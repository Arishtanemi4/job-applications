About the role
Overview
A VC-backed insurtech is hiring a full-stack Product Engineer to build reliable systems that power end-to-end insurance claims processing. The role combines product engineering, operational workflow design, and customer-informed development.

What you will do
Build and own full-stack software powering insurance claim processing.
Work with internal and external users to identify bottlenecks and improve claims workflows.
Ship features across the stack using technologies such as Next.js, Node.js, cloud infrastructure, Postgres, and infrastructure-as-code.
Collaborate directly with founders and a small engineering team.
Help scale a 0-to-1 product in a large, operationally complex market.
What we are looking for
Strong full-stack engineering experience.
Comfortable with modern web frameworks, backend services, databases, and cloud infrastructure.
Excellent communication and product judgement.
Able to operate in ambiguous, high-velocity environments.
High ownership and willingness to work close to users.
