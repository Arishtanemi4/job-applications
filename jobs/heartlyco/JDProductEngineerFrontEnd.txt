About the role
Overview
A VC-backed insurtech is hiring a front-end-leaning Product Engineer to build core systems behind fast, reliable AI-powered insurance claims. The company combines AI and claims expertise to help insurers manage claims from intake through coverage decisions and payments.

What you will do
Build polished, reliable front-end and product experiences for insurance workflows.
Work closely with internal teams and external users to improve claims handling.
Own product features end-to-end, from requirements through launch.
Partner with founders, design, operations, and engineering on high-impact product decisions.
Contribute to a platform that improves speed, accuracy, and compassion in claims handling.
What we are looking for
Strong front-end engineering experience with modern JavaScript/TypeScript frameworks.
Product mindset and interest in building for real operational users.
Comfortable working across the stack when needed.
Excellent communication and ability to operate in ambiguity.
High ownership and bias toward shipping.
